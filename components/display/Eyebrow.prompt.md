Eyebrow — the tracked caps label that opens every section.

```jsx
<Eyebrow line>Global Collaborative Infrastructure</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow number="01">Crossing the Trust Threshold</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow align="center">Join Us</Eyebrow>
```

- Always followed by a light-weight headline (font-weight 300–400, tight tracking). Margin below: 24–40px.
