Team, founder or advisor card for About pages, decks and one-pagers.

```jsx
<PersonCard name="Name Surname" role="Co-founder · Expert Network" bio="Two lines on relevant experience." />
```

- Photos render greyscale to keep the palette quiet; without `photo` a hairline initial tile stands in.
- `layout="horizontal"` for dense lists (advisors). Only list people who have confirmed in writing.
