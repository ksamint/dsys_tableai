15 — Hard questions

What investors ask, answered

Isn't this consulting with an AI label?

Today 85% of revenue is services. But our rules are written, signed, reused across clients and resold as data. Services fall to 30% by 2029.

Why pay US$30K when SaaS costs ¥5K?

They buy a named 30-year operator's judgement in a week. The comparison is a US$50–200K consulting project.

How do four founders reach 70,000 buyers?

We don't. We reach the 70 already in our networks. The Series A funds the sales team beyond them.

What if the bank-IT deal doesn't sign?

The data line falls from US$0.4M to ~US$0.1M in 2028; lines A and B still deliver US$1.8M. Two more vendors are in parallel.

What would prove us wrong: owners refusing to share six months of data. Fifteen clients, including listed companies, already have.
